## Where to Get Help

### Questions and discussion

Open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/RISHII7/night-code/discussions).
Use this for "how do I…", "why does it…", design feedback, and showing off what
you built. Discussions are searchable, so your question helps the next person.

### Bug reports

Open a
[bug report issue](https://github.com/RISHII7/night-code/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).

A good bug report includes the NightCode version, your operating system and
terminal, exact reproduction steps, what you expected, and what actually
happened. Screenshots of the terminal help enormously.

### Feature requests

Open a
[feature request issue](https://github.com/RISHII7/night-code/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

Describe the problem you are trying to solve before proposing a solution. The
most useful feature requests explain a real workflow that is currently painful.

### Security vulnerabilities

**Do not open a public issue.** Follow the coordinated disclosure process in
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

### Commercial support

For enterprise deployments, custom arrangements, or procurement questions,
contact the lead maintainer listed in [`MAINTAINERS.md`](./MAINTAINERS.md).

## What to Expect

This is a small project. Issues and discussions are answered on a best-effort
basis. Security reports are prioritized above everything else — see the response
targets in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).

Please do not:

- Ping maintainers directly for general support questions. Use Discussions so
  the answer benefits everyone.
- Comment "+1" or "any update?" on issues. Use the 👍 reaction instead; it is
  sortable and does not notify every subscriber.
- Open duplicate issues. Search first.

## Helping Others

The fastest way to make this project better is to answer someone else's
question. Contributors who consistently help in Discussions are exactly the
people we invite to become maintainers — see [`GOVERNANCE.md`](./GOVERNANCE.md).
