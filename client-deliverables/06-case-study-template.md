# Case Study Template

_Use this template for every published customer case study. Keep the structure
consistent across case studies so prospects can scan multiple studies quickly
and compare. Replace all bracketed sections with real, verified customer detail
and obtain sign-off from the customer before publishing._

## Internal Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Customer has reviewed and approved the final draft in writing.
- [ ] All metrics have been verified against actual account/usage data, not
      estimated.
- [ ] Quote is attributed to a real, named individual who has approved its use.
- [ ] Any competitor or prior-tool references have been reviewed for accuracy
      and fairness.
- [ ] Legal/compliance sign-off obtained if the customer is in a regulated
      industry.
