# Pricing & Packages

## 1. Pricing Philosophy

NightCode's pricing reflects a simple commitment: **the free tier is the product
for the majority of users, not a funnel.** We price Pro for the minority of
users and teams who need more scale, more throughput, or team-level
administration — not to gate basic usefulness behind a paywall.

## 3. What "Free" Actually Includes

To be explicit, since "free" is frequently met with skepticism in a sales
conversation: the free tier includes full access to every model in the free
catalog, the complete PLAN/BUILD agent experience with no feature gating,
persistent cross-device session history, and a daily allowance sized for real,
everyday development work — not a token trial. There is no time limit on the
free tier and no feature that is artificially withheld from free users to
pressure an upgrade.

## 4. When Pro Makes Sense

- Usage regularly exceeds the daily free allowance (heavy, sustained daily
  development work or automated/scripted usage).
- Consistent low-latency response time matters even during periods of high
  shared-platform load.
- Access to premium, higher-throughput hosted models is valuable for specific
  workloads.
- A team wants centralized billing rather than each member managing an
  individual account.

## 5. Upgrade & Downgrade Mechanics

- Upgrading is self-serve: a single command opens a secure checkout flow, and
  Pro access is active on the very next request — no waiting period.
- Subscriptions can be managed or canceled at any time through a self-serve
  billing portal, with no requirement to contact support.
- Downgrading from Pro to Free at the end of a billing period returns the
  account to the standard daily allowance with no loss of session history —
  nothing is deleted, only the allowance ceiling changes.
